Got Questions?

Frequently Asked
Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most from Utah homeowners and businesses. Don't see yours? Just reach out — we're happy to help.

What areas do you serve?

We proudly serve homes and businesses throughout Utah. If you're not sure whether we cover your area, reach out and we'll let you know right away.

Are you insured and bonded?

Yes. Dastrup Deep Cleaning is fully insured and bonded, and every team member is background-checked before their first visit, so you can feel confident having us in your home or business.

Do I need to provide cleaning supplies or equipment?

No — our team brings all professional-grade cleaning supplies and equipment needed to get the job done. If you have specific products you'd like us to use, just let us know.

How do I get a price quote?

Fill out the form on our Contact page or call us at (801) 207-9056. We'll ask a few quick questions about your space and provide a personalized, no-obligation quote the same day.

What's your satisfaction guarantee?

If any area doesn't meet your expectations, let us know within 24 hours and we'll come back to re-clean it at no additional cost.

Do you offer recurring cleaning plans?

Yes. We offer weekly, bi-weekly, and monthly recurring service for homes and businesses, in addition to one-time deep cleans and move in/move out cleaning.

Will I have the same cleaning team every visit?

For recurring clients, yes — we assign a consistent team so you always know who's coming, rather than a rotating cast of strangers.

Do you clean commercial and specialty spaces?

Absolutely. Beyond residential cleaning, we service offices, medical and dental clinics, gyms, schools, banks, auto dealerships, retail stores, restaurants, and property management common areas. See our full Services list.

Do you require a long-term contract?

No contracts, no long-term commitments, and no surprise fees. You can start, pause, or stop service whenever you need to.

How do I pay for services?

We accept all major payment methods. Your invoice and payment details are confirmed up front with your quote, so there are never any surprises.
